# CONTEXT.md

Living state of the project: what it is, where things are, what is decided, and what is done. Compact and current, not a changelog. Agents: read this first and update it when work materially changes the project (see `AGENTS.md`).

## Overview

Weekly Meeting Notes: an internal website where each person writes their own notes in a Word-like editor, tags parts of them with their projects (each project page shows its parts as updates), and each team meeting shows every note linked to it on one page. Replaces personal Word files collected before meetings. Sign-in through Apps Gateway (company SSO); a first sign-in creates an **inactive** user that a website admin must activate. Design: `PRD & RFC Weekly Meeting Notes.md`. Go-live dossier: `PROJECT.md`.

## Key Decisions

- Every id is a random UUID (owner, 2026-11-23): `gen_random_uuid()` defaults in the database, `idSchema` (`backend/src/utils/ids.ts`, lower-cased) in every route schema, so addresses can't be guessed or counted. Image storage keys from before keep their numeric note folder.
- Quarters (owner, 2026-11-23): like the team's Word file that starts anew each quarter, a team's year is split into periods, set by each team's leader (1, 2, 3, 4, 6 or 12 parts, and the start month; 4 quarters from January by default). Periods are computed from those two numbers and the meetings' days; nothing is stored per period, so a new split moves nothing. Ended periods stay open (late notes still go in) and are marked Ended. A period's page shows its meetings and "Read the whole quarter" (no summary numbers there); the meeting page links to its period, Home shows the current one.
- Languages (owner, 2026-11-24): English (default) and Bahasa Indonesia with i18next, the choice kept in each person's browser (no database column), switched with "ID | EN" in the header; downloads always in English. Only the website's own words are translated, never what people typed; the page names (Home, Notes, Projects, Teams, Users) stay English in both languages. Draft Indonesian wording to be reviewed by the team (catatan, proyek, tim, rapat, ketua tim, kuartal).
- Saving a quarter's notes (owner, 2026-11-24): the reader filters by meetings and people, and "Save as PDF" or "Save as Word" downloads exactly what is shown. Both are built in the browser from the editor JSON, never HTML: Word with `docx` 9.15.3, the PDF with `pdfkit` 0.21.0 (owner, 2026-11-24: download at once instead of the print window; MIT with MIT dependencies, OSV and license checked; pdfmake was not taken because its bundle ships `sax` under BlueOak-1.0.0, which the license policy sends to legal review). Neither is on the tech radar; approval pending in `PROJECT.md`. The PDF uses the 14 standard PDF fonts (nothing embedded), so text outside Windows-1252 is spelled out or left out (`pdfText`). A whole quarter of the team's real data is about 85 MB as Word (14 animated GIFs kept animated) and 26 MB as PDF (144 pages, GIFs as their first frame).
